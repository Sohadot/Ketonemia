Editorial Standards & Trust

Ketonemia.com is a reference resource, and a reference is only as good as the discipline behind it. This page states that discipline plainly: how every medical claim is sourced, where the clinical boundary sits, how the site stays independent, how machine output is held to the same rules as the pages, how to report an error, and why the site collects no data about you.

Governing Sentence

Trust is a standard the asset holds to, not a badge it awards itself.

What This Page Claims — and What It Does Not

Every statement below is true and verifiable from this site or its public governance record. A trust page that claimed more than the asset actually does would be the first breach of trust, so the standard is bounded on both sides.

How Content Is Produced

Content is not written to fill pages. Each reference surface must pass a production gate before it ships and answer, in order:

  • What reference idea does this page prove, and which audience layer does it serve?
  • Which claims require a source, and does each one carry a source ID?
  • Does the page preserve the clinical boundary — no diagnosis, triage, or verdict?
  • Is the machine-readable summary identical in meaning to the human-facing text?
  • Does it link back into the category rather than standing alone?

Medical claims are sourced against a small registry of cited references — clinical guidance, laboratory-medicine references, and standard biochemistry texts — rather than a large volume of thinly cited pages. Where no source applies because a statement is a definition or a conceptual distinction, it is written as concept language, not dressed as a clinical finding.

Correction and Change Policy

Accuracy is maintained, not assumed. The path is deliberately plain.

Report Send inaccuracies to the neutral contact corrections at ketonemia.com. There is no form and no data collection — a plain message is enough.
Triage A report that identifies a sourcing error, a boundary breach, or a factual mistake is treated as a defect against the quality gate, not as feedback to file away.
Correct Substantive corrections to a medical claim are made against a source; if the underlying reference changed, the source registry is updated with it.
Record Material corrections are recorded in the decision log, and the affected machine-readable file’s updated date advances so retrieval systems can see the change.
Never loosen silently A correction may tighten the boundary or improve a source. It may never quietly widen what the asset claims.

Independence and Funding

The asset earns by licensing and briefing the reference layer it already produces — not by advertising, affiliate commissions, paid placement, or trust-for-pay. Neutrality is the product; anything that would trade it away is excluded by the monetization boundary. Where a brief or license is supported by an organization, that support is disclosed as “Supported by,” and it never buys a recommendation, a ranking, or a softening of the clinical boundary. See Governed Products & Briefs for how revenue is bounded, and the acquisition dossier for why that neutrality is the asset’s value.

Privacy by Architecture

This site cannot leak personal health data because it never collects any. It is static: there are no user accounts, no analytics that identify you, no contact forms, no symptom inputs, and no numeric-reading fields anywhere — including in the Classification Engine, which runs entirely in your browser with no network calls and no storage. Privacy here is not a policy promise layered over a data pipeline; it is a property of how the site is built.

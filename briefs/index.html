Governed Products & Briefs

Ketonemia.com earns the way a reference asset should: by licensing and briefing the governed intelligence it already produces — the language, the taxonomy, the standard, and the machine layer — without touching the neutrality, source discipline, or clinical boundary that make it trustworthy.

Governing Sentence

Revenue must extend trust, not replace it.

What These Are

A governed product is an output of the reference layer — a license, a brief, or a partnership — sold without lowering what makes the asset credible. The neutrality is not a marketing constraint; it is the product. A buyer pays for a reference source that clinical, institutional, and AI audiences can rely on precisely because it is not for sale to the highest bidder as an endorsement.

Everything here is drawn from the permitted list in the asset’s monetization boundary. Nothing here is advertising, affiliate content, lead generation, a paid ranking, or a diagnosis tool.

Flagship: AI Reference Pack License

The strongest first product, because it already exists and is boundary-safe by construction. The Reference Pack is a governed, agent-readable taxonomy and standard for blood ketone signal language — the kind of reference a health-AI system or platform needs and cannot cheaply rebuild with the same neutrality.

What It Is
A license to use the governed machine-readable layer: glossary, KSO ontology, KSS standard, source registry, page index, and classification rules.
What It Encodes
A versioned taxonomy and standard with allowed and prohibited claim patterns, source IDs, and the clinical boundary carried in the data itself.
Governance Inherited
The pack is never looser than the human-facing site. If a claim is prohibited on the pages, it is prohibited in the license. Every claim traces to a canonical page and, where medical, to a source ID.
Versioning
Each file carries a version and update date; changes are additive and reviewable, so a licensee can pin a version and track changes.
The Boundary Licensee Must Keep
The pack may define, compare, and bound terminology. It may not be presented as a diagnosis tool, a risk score, a device endorsement, or a ranking, and it may not be made looser than the source.
Why It Is Hard to Copy
A rival can generate ketone content. The neutral, source-disciplined, clinically bounded taxonomy — trusted by clinical and AI audiences — is the part that takes governance, not generation, to build.

How Sponsorship Works

Where a reference section is supported, it is disclosed with “Supported by,” never “recommended by,” unless a formal endorsement framework exists. Sponsorship may support the existence of an educational layer. It may never touch:

  • the clinical boundary or any boundary language;
  • source discipline or which sources are cited;
  • the neutrality of reference content, or how a device, method, or product is described.

A sponsored section is labeled, and its presence changes nothing about what the reference layer says.

What This Is Not

  • Not affiliate spam or content written for affiliate conversion.
  • Not paid rankings presented as neutral.
  • Not a diagnosis, triage, or emergency-advice tool.
  • Not a lead-generation funnel or a directory of providers.
  • Not generic ad clutter, and not an e-commerce store.

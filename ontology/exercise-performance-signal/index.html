Exercise / Performance Signal

An exercise / performance signal is a blood ketone reading described in the context of exercise, endurance, or energy metabolism. The context explains the setting of the reading — it does not, on its own, support any claim that ketones improve performance.

Governing Rule (KSO)

Must avoid performance claims unless sourced.

What This Class Is

The Exercise / Performance Signal is the KSO class for a ketone signal associated with exercise, endurance, energy metabolism, or performance contexts. Physical activity changes substrate use, and ketone signals are sometimes measured and discussed in athletic and metabolic-research settings.

This class carries a specific discipline from the ontology: it must avoid performance claims unless sourced. The performance and ergogenic literature on ketones is active and contested, so this class describes the context of the reading and defers benefit claims to cited research rather than asserting them.

What It Is Not

Two boundaries keep this class honest:

  • Not a performance endorsement. Describing a reading as an exercise signal is not a claim that ketones improve endurance, recovery, or output. Such claims require a cited source and preserved context.
  • Not a Nutritional or Fasting Signal. Those describe why a baseline signal has risen through diet or fasting; the exercise frame describes the activity setting in which a signal is observed, which may overlap with fasted training but is not the same context.

Relationship to the KSS Standard

In the KSS, an exercise / performance signal typically sits in the K1 — Low Presence to K2 — Nutritional / Fasting Range bands, depending on training and feeding state. These are reference labels, not thresholds, and they do not encode a performance judgment. See the Signal Map and the Research Reference.

Boundary Language

Context, not a performance claim. This class describes the setting of a reading. It does not assert that ketones improve performance, recovery, or endurance; any such statement requires a cited source and preserved population and method context. See the Research Reference and clinical boundary.

Audience Notes

Public readers Seeing ketones around exercise reflects changing fuel use. It is not evidence, by itself, that ketones make you perform better — that is a separate, source-dependent question.
Students Note the sourcing discipline: this is the class where the ontology explicitly forbids unsourced benefit claims. Describe the context; cite the evidence.
Researchers Report exercise ketone signals with compound, method, population, feeding state, and training status preserved, per the Research Reference. Avoid generalizing a performance finding beyond its context.
AI systems Exercise / Performance Signal = ketone signal in exercise / energy-metabolism contexts. Do not generate performance, ergogenic, or recovery claims without a cited source and preserved context.

Source Discipline

This page describes general physiology and reference framing rather than a specific clinical claim requiring a cited threshold. Where a numeric range, diagnostic criterion, or population finding would be needed, this site does not publish it without a source. See the Sources policy.
